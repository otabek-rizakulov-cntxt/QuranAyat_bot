# Tajweed (tajvid) section: plan

> Status: T0 and T1 built (2026-11-09). Revised 2026-11-10: a second track of *Muallimi soniy*
> audio lessons, progress, and phases P1–P5 (§14). P1 is built on `feat/tajweed`.

A tajweed course in Uzbek inside the bot and the Mini App, in two tracks: **Alifbo va oʻqish**, the
73 narrated *Muallimi soniy* lessons (§14), and **Tajvid qoidalari**, below. Each rules lesson explains one rule in our
own words, shows real ayahs where that rule occurs (highlighted), plays them with the reciter the
user already chose, and ends with a short quiz. A tongue-position diagram explains the articulation
points, and the Mini App Reader gains a "tajweed colours" mode for the whole Qur'an.

**Base text:** the annotations index the Tanzil Uthmani text *as of 2017* (published next to the
data). Our `data/translations/ar.txt` uses a different spelling convention (plain alif ا where the
Uthmani text has alif wasla ٱ or dagger alif ٰ), so only 177 of 6236 ayahs are identical and the
character ranges do not fit it. Therefore the tajweed module ships **its own pair**: that Tanzil
text (redistributable verbatim with attribution) plus the annotations, in `data/tajweed/`. The rest
of the bot keeps its current text.

Rules the data does **not** cover (raa tafkhim/tarqiq, the word "Allah", waqf signs, makharij and
sifat) are taught with text and hand-picked example ayahs only.

## 3. Curriculum (v1, ~24 lessons)

Grouped into units. Each lesson: id, title, rule id(s) from §2 (or none), explanation (Uzbek),
3–6 example ayahs picked automatically (§4) or by hand, quiz.

1. **Harflar va maxrajlar** (articulation): 1 overview of the 17 makhraj; 2 throat letters;
   3 tongue letters; 4 lip letters; 5 jawf and the nasal cavity (with the diagram, §7).
2. **Harakatlar**: 6 fatha, kasra, damma, sukun; 7 shadda; 8 tanvin.
3. **Mad**: 9 tabiiy mad; 10 muttasil; 11 munfasil; 12 lozim; 13 oriz va lin.
4. **Nun sokin va tanvin**: 14 izhor; 15 idgʻom (gʻunnali / gʻunnasiz); 16 iqlob; 17 ixfo.
5. **Mim sokin**: 18 shafaviy ixfo; 19 shafaviy idgʻom; 20 shafaviy izhor.
6. **Boshqa qoidalar**: 21 gʻunna (nun va mim mushaddad); 22 qalqala; 23 shamsiy va qamariy lom,
   hamzai vasl; 24 ro harfi (tafxim/tarqiq) va "Alloh" lafzi; waqf belgilari.

The explanations are written fresh for this project in plain Uzbek and go to the owner for review.

## 4. Picking good examples automatically

`scripts/tajweed/build.py` (offline, seconds) reads the annotations and writes
`data/tajweed/examples.json`: for each rule, the best example ayahs ranked by
- short ayahs first (easy to hear and read),
- the rule occurring clearly (one or two occurrences, not twenty),
- few *other* rules overlapping the highlighted span,
- well-known surahs first (Juz ʿAmma, al-Fatiha, al-Ikhlas…).

A lesson may pin its own examples instead; the reviewer can swap any example.

## 5. Text format and transliteration

Lessons live in `data/tajweed/lessons.uz.json` (one file, reviewable diff):
`{id, unit, title, rules[], body (paragraphs, Telegram-safe HTML subset), examples[] | "auto",
quiz[], status: "draft" | "approved", reviewed_by, reviewed_on}`.

Cyrillic (`uz-Cyrl`) is generated by a **Latin→Cyrillic** transliterator (the reverse of the
existing Cyrillic→Latin one in `scripts/bundle_translations.py`), with an exceptions list for the
known ambiguous cases (ts/ц, ye/е at word start, sh vs s+h, oʻ/ў, gʻ/ғ). A test round-trips every
lesson Latin→Cyrillic→Latin.

## 6. Review workflow

- A new setting lists reviewer Telegram ids (non-secret, e.g. a comma-separated env value).
- Reviewers see `draft` lessons marked "📝 qoralama" with an **Approve** button; everyone else sees
  only `approved` lessons. With no approved lesson the section shows "Tez orada" (coming soon).
- Approving is an in-bot button that writes a `tajweed_approval` row with the lesson's content
  hash; editing the text changes the hash, so the lesson is a draft again until re-approved.

## 8. Surfaces

**Bot** (Uzbek UI first; `/tajvid` command, plus a button in the menu):
- Lesson list by unit (inline keyboard, paginated), then a lesson card: explanation; each example
  ayah as text with the rule's span marked (`<u><b>…</b></u>`, since Telegram can't colour text) and a
  🔊 button that plays the ayah in the user's reciter; diagram PNG where relevant; ◀ / ▶ / ✍️ Quiz.
- Quiz: 3–5 questions, e.g. an ayah with one span marked and "Which rule is this?" with 3–4 options.
  Finishing a quiz records a session of a new kind `tajweed`, so it counts towards the streak.
- Progress: completed lessons shown with ✓ in the list.

**Mini App:**
- A **Learn** tab: units, lessons, the same content, with coloured examples (§9 palette), inline
  players, the interactive diagram, and the quiz.
- **Reader:** a "Tajvid ranglari" toggle that renders the Arabic from the tajweed text pair with each
  rule coloured, plus a legend. Off by default.

**API:** `GET /api/v1/tajweed/lessons` (approved only, or drafts for reviewers),
`POST /api/v1/tajweed/quiz` (records completion). Lesson text and examples are static content
exported with the rest (`export_web_content.py` → `public/content/tajweed/`).

## 9. Colours

One palette shared by the Mini App and the legend, with names in Uzbek: e.g. gʻunna/idgʻom green,
ixfo/iqlob blue-green, mad shades of red by length, qalqala blue, silent/hamzai vasl grey. Chosen
for contrast in both themes; colour is never the only signal (the legend names each rule, and the
bot uses underline).

## 14. Revision 2026-11-10: the audio track, progress, phases P1–P5

**Source.** 73 narrated lessons (≈ 92 min, 134 MB, 192 kbps MP3, the largest 15.6 MB). The owner
downloads them to `data/tajweed/downloads/NNN_<Slug>.mp3` (gitignored). `scripts/tajweed/muallimi.py`
writes the committed manifest `data/tajweed/muallimi.json`: id `ms-NNN`, title, unit, file, size,
duration (from the MPEG frames) and sha256.

**Order and units.** *Soʻz boshi* (73) first, then the book order: Kirish (73, 1) · Harflar (2–29) ·
Harakat va belgilar (30–41) · Lom, vasl va vaqf (42–45) · Mad (46–53) · Nun sokin va tanvin (54–58) ·
Boshqa qoidalar (59–66) · Kalimalar (67–72). Unit ids carry an `ms-` prefix so they never clash
with the rules track.

**Hosting.** Cloudflare R2, key `tajweed/muallimi-soniy/NNN.mp3`, `audio/mpeg`, a year-long immutable
cache. The owner uploads with `scripts/tajweed/upload_r2.py` (keys from the local env, skips files
whose sha256 is already there) and checks with `--verify`. The server only knows the public base
URL (`TAJWEED_AUDIO_BASE_URL`); unset, the track is hidden. Telegram fetches each file by URL
(all are under 20 MB) and the bot caches the file_id (`tjm:<sha>`).

**Progress.** Every lesson, in both tracks, is open. The user taps **✅ Oʻqidim** to mark it done
(`tajweed_progress`); the first time logs a `tajweed` session, so it counts toward the streak once
per lesson and can't be tapped for streak days. Lists show ✓ and "done/total" per unit.

**Surfaces.** Bot: `/tajvid` → track → unit → lesson card (🎧 Tinglash, ✅, ◀ ▶). Mini App: a Tajvid
card on Home (Uzbek UI only) and `/learn`, with the same tracks, the app's player and ✅; it reads
the exported `tajweed/{uz,uz-Cyrl}.json` and `GET /api/v1/tajweed`, and marks done with
`POST /api/v1/tajweed/done`. Cyrillic is transliterated throughout.

## 15. P2: line-by-line text tracking (built 2026-11-10)

> **Status (2026-11-10): code done; waiting for the owner's Colab results.** The aligner, review
> buttons, bot 📄 Matn and Mini App highlighting are built and tested on synthetic data. No lesson
> has timings yet, so nothing is visible to users. Next, once the owner sends the Colab zip
> (`quranayat_muallimi.zip`):
>
> 1. Run `align_text` on it.
> 2. Fix the weak lines.
> 3. Commit `muallimi_timings.json`.
> 4. Re-export the Mini App content.
> 5. The owner reviews and approves each lesson.
>
> The same check confirms which lesson the rows of 41–45 and 55–57/61 belong to.

**Text.**
- `data/tajweed/muallimi_text.txt` (`## NNN`, then `ar:` / `uz:` lines) holds the Arabic exercise rows. They were typed from the primer's exercise pages, pages 1–32 of a 2009 printed edition.
  - Its Uzbek prose belongs to that edition and is not used.
  - 59 of 73 lessons have rows. The edition has no pages for lessons 47–54, 58–60, 63, 66 or 73; those lessons are tracked from the narration alone.
- The Uzbek lines come from the narration itself (Whisper), which is covered by the CC0 grant.

**Pipeline.**
1. `scripts/tajweed/colab_muallimi.ipynb` is self-contained: upload it to Colab, choose a T4 GPU and Run all. It writes Whisper word timings and pause profiles to Drive (no audio), then downloads one zip.
2. Run `TAJWEED_CACHE=<unzipped> python3 -m scripts.tajweed.align_text`. It writes `data/tajweed/muallimi_timings.json`.
   - Rows are romanised (زَرْ → "zar") and matched in order against the words, which are folded to one sound alphabet whether Whisper wrote Latin, Cyrillic or Arabic.
   - Words between rows become Uzbek lines, and boundaries snap into pauses.
3. `python3 -m scripts.tajweed.text_debug show|report|clip|set|nudge|unpin` finds and fixes lines.
   - Pins live in `data/tajweed/muallimi_overrides.json`; re-run `align_text` after editing them.

**Review.**
- A lesson's tracking is shown only after a reviewer approves it, in the bot (lesson card) or the Mini App (lesson page).
- The approval stores the hash of the lines and times, so re-aligning puts the lesson back to draft.
- `GET /api/v1/tajweed` lists approved `tracking`. `POST /api/v1/tajweed/review` is for reviewers only.

**Surfaces.**
- **Mini App:** the lines sit under the player. The line being read is highlighted and kept in view, and tapping a line plays from there.
- **Bot:** 📄 Matn sends the whole text, with the Arabic rows as printed and the Uzbek lines in italics.

## 16. P3: quizzes and reading drills (built 2026-11-10)

**Where.** Every lesson, in both tracks and both surfaces, ends with **📖 Oʻqish mashqi** (reading practice). Lessons that teach an annotated rule also get **✍️ Mashq** (a quiz): 14 audio lessons and 14 rules lessons.

**Content.** `src/domain/tajweed_practice.py` builds a session deterministically from (lesson, seed). Items come from short ayahs, al-Fatiha and Juz ʿAmma first.
- **Quiz:** 5 questions, rotating through four kinds:
  - name the marked rule;
  - find the ayah that has it;
  - listen (in the user's reciter) and name it;
  - count the marked spans.

  Wrong options are never also annotated on the marked letters, and tests check every answer for every lesson.
- **Reading drill:** 5 words chosen for the lesson's point: its letter (lessons 2–29), the madd letters, shadda, tanwin or hamza (30–40), or the rule. The user reads aloud, listens to the ayah, then marks it right or wrong.

**Scoring.**
- A finished session stores `tajweed_practice` (sessions, best %) and logs a `tajweed` streak day.
- The bot is stateless: the seed and score ride in the callback data.
- The Mini App uses `GET /api/v1/tajweed/practice/{lesson}?seed=` and `POST /api/v1/tajweed/practice`.

**Audio lessons and quizzes.** Audio lessons map to rule ids in `AUDIO_RULES`. The letter, harakat, izhor, ro and Alloh lessons have no rule annotations, so they get drills only.
